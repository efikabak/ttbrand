02 Clear space

Room toBreathe

½X½X½X½X
X
Keep ½X on every side

X is the full width of the symbol. Keep that half-width clear around every lockup. No type, images, or edges inside it.

Symbol: 24px minimum

Smaller than that, the sword and shield blur together. Favicons are the one exception.

Clean, solid backgrounds

Obsidian, Ash, Golden Yellow or a brand neutral. If the background is busy, the mark loses.

06 Templates

Built forThe feed

The Instagram system, at 1080 × 1440. Start from these and the post already looks like us.

The rules

  • Cover headlines in Trove, uppercase, big and tight.
  • Gold for one thing: the number, the name, the record.
  • Inside slides put the headline on gold blocks over the grid.
  • Details in Archivo, uppercase and small, near the bottom.
  • The mark sits bottom center, small and quiet.
  • Obsidian ground with grain. Never flat black.
  • 3:4 for posts, 9:16 for reel covers. Keep text in the middle 3:4.
